Welcome to Sky Plaza.

Drive up the orange ramps to the green terrace, then up the wide central ramp to the gold sky deck. Pink pads launch you off the sides. Every kart and shell respects height: an attack on one floor cannot hit a driver below it.

Each driver has three balloons. Collect a blue ? box to get one shell, banana, or boost. Bots have to collect them too. Empty boxes return after five seconds with everyone clear of them. In co-op, teammates cannot hurt each other.

Host a room and send its code or invite link. Your friend joins as Luigi, with their own camera. Keep the host tab open and visible. The host can restart a finished battle; eliminated drivers spectate. Rotate phones sideways for more driving room. The overview button lets you inspect all three floors.

This is a custom 3D game. Play the original SNES game here. Room codes belong to the mode you opened.
